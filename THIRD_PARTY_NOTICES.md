# Third-party inputs and method attributions

This archive distributes processed tables, metadata, and figures generated by
the repository authors. The external benchmark Hamiltonian files and raw
third-party datasets described below are not redistributed in this archive.
The repository licenses therefore apply to the authors' original code and
processed outputs, not to those external inputs.

## External benchmark inputs

### Equilibrium N2/cc-pVDZ benchmark

The N2 selected-space calculations use the all-electron cc-pVDZ Hamiltonian at
an internuclear distance of 2.118 bohr associated with:

Z. Wang, F. Li, and H. Lu, "Coordinate Descent Full Configuration
Interaction," *Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation* **15**, 3558
(2019), https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.9b00138.

The source FCIDUMP is not included. The released tables contain the authors'
DMRG, supervised-learning, and analysis results derived from that benchmark.

### Fe2S2 CAS(30e,20o) benchmark

The Fe2S2 selected-space calculations use the QianKunNet benchmark
CAS(30e,20o), M_S=0 Hamiltonian described in:

- H. Ma, B. Kan, H. Shang, and J. Yang, "Transformer-Based Neural Networks
  Backflow for Strongly Correlated Electronic Structure," arXiv:2509.25720
  (2025), https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2509.25720.
- Z. Wu, B. Zhang, W.-H. Fang, and Z. Li, "Hybrid Tensor Network and Neural
  Network Quantum States for Quantum Chemistry," *Journal of Chemical Theory
  and Computation* **21**, 10252 (2025),
  https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.5c01228.

The source FCIDUMP and external wave-function files are not included. The
released tables contain the authors' DMRG, supervised-learning, and analysis
results derived from the benchmark.

## Literature-derived ansatz definitions

The matrix-product backflow construction follows G. Lami, G. Carleo, and M.
Collura, "Matrix product states with backflow correlations," *Physical Review
B* **106**, L081111 (2022),
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.106.L081111. The repository does not
redistribute numerical data from that article.

Other scientific software and literature methods used to generate the
processed results are cited in the accompanying manuscript and provenance
metadata. Their names and citations do not imply that their authors endorse
this archive.
